# Leadping

> Leadping helps teams respond to new inbound leads fast with SMS, voice, alerts, and follow-up workflows.

Leadping helps organizations move new inbound leads from source to first touch, organization handoff, conversation history, and follow-up without stitching together separate tools. This file contains public product and documentation links only.

## Billing Policy Summary

Purchased prepaid usage credits expire 12 months after purchase, have no cash value, are not deposits, gift cards, escrow, or stored-value accounts, and are non-refundable except where required by law. Credits are only usable for eligible Leadping services and are not transferable unless Leadping expressly allows it. Carrier, registration, telecom, phone-number, domain/website, pass-through, subscription, and consumed usage charges may follow separate refund rules and are generally non-refundable after submission, provisioning, or use. Unauthorized charges or suspected account compromise should be reported to support for review.
